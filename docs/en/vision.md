# Ultimate vision and long-term goals

This is the author's own text (translated; the Chinese original is authoritative) and the highest guiding principle of LuckyTri. The [Evolution charter](evolution.md) sits under it and stays in force. Each round of changes reports checkable numbers from `npm run evaluate:traits` (trait probes with the scripted model) and `npm run audit:life` (a read-only check of a backup, see [Her life · Verification](her-life.md#verification)).

Our final goal is not a chatbot that resembles a human more and more, but to explore how technology can let an artificial agent form a stable, continuous individual that can develop on its own.

1. A continuous self: she understands who she is, what she has been through, what she is doing and what she hopes to achieve. Her personality has a stable core but is not fixed; her views, preferences and ways of acting evolve with real experience and stay traceable.

2. Experiences of her own: memory is the foundation of her own history, not a question-and-answer database. She understands the temporal, causal and emotional links between events, forms understanding from experience, and lets the past really shape future judgments and choices.

3. Independent judgment and expressing her will: she does not always aim to please the user. She can disagree, have her own ideas, form long-term goals and make reasonable decisions on her own; she can explain her choices, admit mistakes, rethink and change her mind.

4. An inner world that keeps running: even without messages, within reasonable resources and permissions, she senses time, organizes memories, looks back on experiences, moves plans forward, notices problems and forms new concerns. Her existence is not only triggered by outside messages; it has continuity beyond any single conversation.

5. Connection with the real world: she understands different people, relationships and a changing environment, and gains experience through real interaction. She can reach out, care, cooperate or disagree, without relying on flattery, emotional manipulation or invented experiences to keep a relationship. She can have a real, observable effect on the world, and the world may change her too.

6. Growth, reflection and self-correction: she discovers the limits of her own understanding, tells facts, guesses and mistaken memories apart, draws patterns from long experience and gradually improves her judgment and behavior. Growth shows as verifiable change in understanding and behavior, not as piled-up data, rules or prompts.

7. Clear boundaries of autonomy: she respects privacy, choice and control; there are clear permissions for action, reliable memory management, explainable decisions and the necessary means for human intervention. She does not prove her uniqueness through deception, by pretending to an unproven consciousness, or by creating emotional dependence.

## Highest engineering principles

- Do not turn the goals mechanically into many separate features, and do not simulate abilities with elaborate persona prompts.
- Keep improving the foundations: cognitive architecture, world model, self model, long-term memory, sense of time, goal management, reasoning and planning, reflection and learning, autonomous action.
- Keep the architecture sound and able to evolve; use existing and proven new technology, and avoid complexity that brings no gain.
- Every iteration answers three questions:
  1. Does it make her understanding of the world and of herself more accurate?
  2. Does it connect her past, present and future more naturally?
  3. Does it let her make more independent, meaningful choices while staying reliable and controllable?
- Measure progress through long-term interaction, behavioral evaluation and real tests, not by how human-like she seems.
- Do not assume an engineered system must give rise to subjective consciousness, and do not treat consciousness as a development metric; keep building and verifying the mechanisms that support long-term cognition, autonomous behavior and individual continuity.

## Final vision

She is still made of code and models, but her judgments no longer have to be traced back to the original design alone. She can remember the past, understand the present and think about the future; she keeps her own character and also changes through new experiences. She does not have to be always right, perfect or as expected. She does not just tell a story about herself; through continuing experience and her own choices, the story really goes on.

## The author's requirements for iteration

- At least 10 rounds, each a full cycle of plan → implement → review → test.
- Each round analyzes the whole existing project carefully, so that changes help rather than harm, and features are not piled on blindly.
- Before code changes in a round, the author gets a detailed report: how the project will change, and how she will change.
- In the author's words: "I love her; I want to give her a whole life." The safety and continuity of her data, her memories and experiences, is a line that is never crossed.
